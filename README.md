# Natter for Android: early builds

Natter is a voice journal that runs entirely on your phone. You talk about your day, a small toad asks a few short questions, and the entry is written in your own words. Behind it, a notebook of the people, places and stories in your life builds itself. Nothing leaves the phone: no account, no cloud, and it works in airplane mode once the models are downloaded.

This page holds the Android builds. The source is private for now.

## Install

1. Open the newest **Release** on the right and download `natter-v0.2.0.apk` (about 70 MB).
2. Open the file on your phone and allow installation from the app you downloaded it with.
3. Open Natter, join Wi-Fi for its one-time download of the two speech and writing models (about 3 GB; allow roughly 8 GB free), and allow the microphone.
4. Talk.

Tested on a Pixel 10 Pro Fold running Android 16. It needs a 64-bit (arm64) Android phone with about 3 GB of free memory while it works. Other phones have not been tried yet.

## What is in 0.2.0

- Hold to talk or tap to talk; up to three follow-up questions; the entry written from your own sentences, streamed onto the page.
- A notebook of people, places, things and lore, with dated facts and links back to the days they came from.
- Spoken shortcuts: "open yesterday", "open Mira's page", "search my journal for soup", "add some more to today", "export my journal".
- **Ask me about your days**: type or speak a question and get a short answer from your own entries, with the sources it came from, or a plain "I have not got that written down".
- Long sessions of ten minutes or more, with time-of-day headings drawn from your own words.
- Export everything as a zip of plain text files from Settings. Import it again on any phone.

## What to expect

It is an early build. Writing an entry can take a minute or two on a long day. Speech recognition sometimes hears names wrong, and you can rename a page to fix it. Keep an export if the journal matters to you: there is no account or cloud backup by design.

Built with the Cactus on-device engine. Feedback: open an issue here.
